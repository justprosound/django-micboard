### Changed

- Route the poll, realtime and API-server paths through one identity-safe chassis ingest that owns the identity lock, deduplication, move detection and lifecycle, with partial-event and authoritative-snapshot modes.

### Fixed

- Stop realtime and API-server updates from creating a duplicate chassis for hardware already known under another serial, MAC address or API id, or owned by another manufacturer.
- Keep a chassis address when a realtime event omits it instead of overwriting it with an empty value.
